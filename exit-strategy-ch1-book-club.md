# Book Club

## Chapter 1

### The Intercepted Arrival

The chapter opens with Murderbot returning to HaveRatton Station aboard its transport, Ship. Its paranoia proves justified when the Port Authority diverts Ship toward a dock where a heavily armed Palisade security team is waiting. Murderbot correctly deduces the corporate mercenaries are hunting for a rogue SecUnit and immediately begins calculating a bypass.

### The Airlock Escape & Log Scrubbing

To avoid the ambush, Murderbot executes a flawless stealth exit. It dons the evac suit it stole in the previous book, exits through the opposite airlock, and scrubs its presence from Ship’s logs. By hacking a station cargo bot and exploiting a logic gap—bots are programmed to stop theft, but not to ignore polite requests from other bots—it sneaks into an empty cargo module to safely enter the station.

### System Bypasses and Neural Upgrades

Once inside, Murderbot must hide both its internal energy weapons and the modified, armor-piercing projectile weapon it is carrying. It hacks HaveRatton’s weapon-scanning drones to blind them to its presence. During this hack, Murderbot realizes that the massive processing load it handled back on Milu actually forced its organic brain to forge new neural pathways, making complex system overrides much easier. It is literally evolving through hard work.

### Choosing an Identity

To further disguise its SecUnit profile, Murderbot uses ART’s old code templates to accelerate its hair growth. It then navigates a highly anxiety-inducing environment: an automated retail shop. For the first time, Murderbot selects and purchases its own clothing. It experiences the strange, novel sensation of having a "preference" for something other than downloaded media.

### The TranRollinHyfa Revelation

While blending into the station mall, Murderbot connects to the public newsfeed to locate Dr. Mensah so it can deliver the decrypted evidence clips. It uncovers a terrifying twist: GrayCris has aggressively flipped the narrative, accusing Dr. Mensah of corporate espionage. Worse, Mensah is missing and believed to be on TranRollinHyfa—a heavily fortified corporate hub. Murderbot realizes Mensah hasn't gone there voluntarily; GrayCris has trapped her, likely as a direct response to Murderbot's actions on Milu.
