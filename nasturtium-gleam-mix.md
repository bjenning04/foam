# Nasturtium Gleam Mix

- **Life Cycle**: Annual
- **Color**: Mixed
- **Plant Height**: Up to 60 inches
- **Planting Season**: Spring
- **Growth Habit**: Climbing
- **Bloom Season**: Summer
- **Plant Characteristics**
  - Attracts bees
  - Attracts butterflies
  - Attracts hummingbirds
  - Attracts pollinators
  - Easy to grow & maintain
  - Edible
  - Fragrant
- **Use**
  - Container garden
  - Ground cover
  - Hanging basket
  - Weed suppressor
- **Growing Conditions**
  - Drought tolerant
  - Outdoor
  - Poor soil tolerant
- **Plant Resistance**
  - Deer
