# Abs Diet Super Food Oatmeal
## Ingredients
- 3/4 cup oatmeal
- 1 cup milk
- 1 egg
- 1/2 cup berries
- 1 tbsp chopped almonds
- 1 tsp protein powder
- t tsp ground flax seeds
- 1 tbsp nonfat plain yogurt
- 1/2 banana, sliced

## Directions
1. Mix the first seven ingredients and microwave for 2 minutes.
2. Stir and add the second two.
